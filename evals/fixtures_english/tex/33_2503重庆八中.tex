\par The pea incident took place at the Biltmore Hotel, which, to my eight-year-old mind, was just about the fanciest place to eat in the whole world. I grandly ordered a Salisbury steak, confident in the knowledge that beneath that fancy name was a good old hamburger with gravy (肉汁). \par I do not like peas now. I did not like peas then. I have always hated peas. It is a complete mystery to me why anyone would voluntarily eat peas. I did not eat them at home. I did not eat them at restaurants. And I certainly was not about to eat them now.\par
